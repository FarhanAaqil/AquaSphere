# Model Card — AquaSphere (OceanEmbed)

> **Model Architecture:** Multi-Sensor Satellite Embedding & Calibrated Subsurface Decoder  
> **Problem Statement:** SIH 26066 — Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES) / INCOIS  
> **Domain:** North Indian Ocean ($5^\circ\text{–}30^\circ\text{N}, 45^\circ\text{–}105^\circ\text{E}$), 15 Depths ($0\text{–}1000\text{ m}$)  
> **Version:** 2.1.0 (served checkpoint `tier_a_v2.0`, protocol v2)

---

## 1. Intended Use

- **Primary Use:** Decision-support and scientific research tool for oceanographers, climate researchers, and INCOIS analysts to reconstruct continuous subsurface temperature structures from multi-sensor satellite surface fields.
- **Secondary Use:** Marine heatwave subsurface propagation monitoring, upper-ocean heat content estimation, and barrier layer analysis.
- **Out-of-Scope / Misuse:** Not certified for direct tactical submarine navigation, safety-critical offshore engineering, or operational maritime search-and-rescue without independent INCOIS oceanographic validation.

---

## 2. Served Model: Tier A v2

- **Inputs:** 18 values per point: lat, lon, sin/cos(day of year), SST, SSS, SLA, geostrophic u/v, wind u/v, and an availability flag per sensor (a missing sensor reads as the training mean with its flag at 0).
- **Target:** anomaly against a monthly 2° climatology (falling back to 5°, then basin-month) fitted on 2015-2022 profiles only; the climatology is added back.
- **Network:** residual MLP encoder to a 16-d unit-norm embedding, heteroscedastic decoder giving mean and log-variance at 15 depths. Masked heteroscedastic loss with a 5-epoch MSE warm-up and a 2x weight at 50-150 m. The gradient and anti-inversion terms were ablated on validation and left out (no measurable difference, see `docs/results/phase4.md`).
- **Modes:** one model serves `delayed` (all sensors) and `nowcast` (SST + SLA only). During training 30% of samples are drawn in nowcast mode and each non-SST sensor is dropped with p = 0.1.
- **Intervals:** split-conformal 90% intervals, Mondrian by region (Arabian Sea / Bay of Bengal, split at 78°E) x monsoon season, per depth, calibrated separately for each mode on 2023. Strata with fewer than 30 scores at a depth fall back to the region, then to all strata.
- **Applicability domain:** dissimilarity index DI = distance to the nearest training embedding / mean nearest-neighbour distance among training embeddings; threshold 2.69 (upper whisker of training-fold DI). Every prediction reports `di` and `in_domain`.
- **Tiers B and C (evaluated, not shipped):** a 9x9 patch CNN over 4 lags (t, t-7, t-14, t-28 days) x 7 channels plus a land channel; Tier C adds self-supervised pre-training on 2015-2022 patches (masked reconstruction, and masked + temporal forecasting).

## 3. Training & Evaluation Splits

- **Temporal:** train 2015-2022, validation and calibration 2023, test 2024-2025.
- **Float-grouped:** each float belongs to the first period it appears in; its later profiles are dropped. Early stopping uses 10% of training floats held out by float.
- **Test period:** locked (`aquasphere.eval.protocol`); it has not been read. The confirmatory hypotheses were frozen beforehand (`docs/hypotheses.yaml`).

## 6. Uncertainty Calibration

- **Method:** Mondrian split-conformal by region x monsoon season, per depth and per mode, calibrated on 2023 (section 2).
- **Coverage estimate:** 89.6% (95% CI 87.6-91.3%) for nominal 90%, measured with cross-conformal folds over floats so no float is scored by a calibrator that saw it. Raw heteroscedastic sigma under-covers (82.3%).
- **Confirmatory test:** H3 (87-93% overall, within 5 points per stratum) is tested once on 2024-2025.

---

## 7. Known Failure Modes

1. **Near-Coastline Masking ($<50\text{ km}$):** Land contamination degrades microwave radiometer footprint for sea surface salinity and altimeter sea level anomalies.
2. **Extreme River Discharge Regimes:** During the post-monsoon (October–November), the Ganges-Brahmaputra plume creates shallow freshwater barrier layers ($<15\text{ m}$) that can decouple surface SST from subsurface heat content.
3. **Thin Bay of Bengal strata:** 12-14 validation floats per season; coverage there is estimated with wide intervals and claims are descriptive only.
4. **Depths Exceeding $700\text{ m}$:** Ocean dynamics become weakly coupled to surface forcing; model predictions revert toward deep climatological priors ($3.6^\circ\text{C}$).

DEPTH 50–150 M · THE THERMOCLINE BARRIER

The Ocean's Thermal Engine

Satellites only observe the ocean skin (top few millimeters). Beneath the surface lies the thermocline—the sharp boundary separating warm upper water from cold abyssal depths.

In the North Indian Ocean, internal waves, monsoon winds, and tropical cyclones depend directly on thermocline dynamics. AquaSphere captures this hidden stratum without requiring physical buoys at every coordinate.

DEPTH 500 M · AUTONOMOUS ARGO OBSERVATIONS

In-Situ Ground Truth

Autonomous robotic Argo floats drift with deep oceanic currents at 1000m, descending to 2000m every 10 days before ascending to transmit true CTD temperature and salinity profiles to satellites.

We trained and evaluated AquaSphere using float-blocked splits—ensuring the model is tested strictly on floats whose trajectories were never seen during training.

DEPTH 1000 M · THE ABYSSAL BOUNDARY

Guaranteed Physical Stratification

Our physics-informed loss function mathematically penalizes unstable density inversions ($\mathcal{L}_{strat}$), guaranteeing that reconstructed temperature columns obey gravitational stability.

Scientific Integrity: The E1 Spatial Leakage Gap

Why random splitting produces misleading, overly optimistic oceanographic metrics

In oceanography, consecutive profiles from the same float are separated by merely 10 days and ~30 km, creating strong spatial and temporal autocorrelation. If profiles from the same float appear in both training and test sets (random splitting), the model simply memorizes the float's trajectory rather than learning the physical link between surface satellites and depth.

Deep Learning Architecture & Physics Formulations

Continuous vertical coordinate queries via cross-attention with gravitational stratification penalties

1. Gravitational Stratification Loss Formulation:
ℒ = ℒ_{NLL} + λ_{strat} ℒ_{strat} + λ_{smooth} ℒ_{smooth}

where ℒ_{strat} = (1 / K-1) ∑ max(0, ∂T / ∂z)² (penalizes non-physical density inversions)
and ℒ_{smooth} = (1 / K-2) ∑ (∂²T / ∂z²)² (enforces vertical profile continuity)

2. Continuous Depth Token Query: Depth levels $z$ are embedded continuously into latent tokens using high-frequency sinusoidal encodings:

PE(z, 2i) = sin(z / 10000^{2i/d}),   PE(z, 2i+1) = cos(z / 10000^{2i/d})

3. Multi-Head Cross-Attention Decoder: A 4-head cross-attention mechanism queries the satellite latent representation with continuous depth tokens, decoding the vertical thermal column with heteroscedastic uncertainty estimation.
